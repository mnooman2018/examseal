# ExamSeal Judge Q&A

## Why not a normal database?
A database controlled by the same organisation could allow evidence to be rewritten.
ExamSeal records commitments and release events on MST.

## What if 3 custodians collude?
They can open the paper early.
ExamSeal does not claim to be leak-proof.
The release requires multiple custodians and every release is recorded.

## Why are encrypted copies on-chain?
The copies stored on-chain are AES-256 ciphertext.
The plaintext paper and AES key are not stored on-chain.

## What if someone retypes the paper?
Question order, option order and wording can still provide attribution.
However, deliberate rewriting and reshuffling can defeat attribution.

## Does a leak from one centre expose everyone?
The exam questions are shared, so a leak can expose the paper.
ExamSeal's purpose is to establish where the leaked copy came from.

## Who created the paper?
The exam authority is trusted when the paper is created.
ExamSeal protects the paper after it is sealed.

## Is the demo codebook special?
Yes.
The demo reuses one codebook so printed photos match across fresh demo exams.
Production exams would use a new secret codebook.

## Does attribution identify a person?
No.
The system identifies the centre associated with the leaked copy.
