# ExamSeal Pitch Deck

## Slide 1 — Hook
A leaked exam paper is circulating on WhatsApp.
Where did it come from?

## Slide 2 — Problem
Exam papers must stay sealed before the exam.
When one leaks, finding the source is difficult.

## Slide 3 — Lock
3-of-5 custodians.
Time-locked release.
Every release is recorded on MST.

## Slide 4 — Trace
Every centre receives a unique paper variant.
A leaked photo can be traced back to its centre.

## Slide 5 — Why MST
Commitments, releases, evidence and revocations are recorded on-chain.
